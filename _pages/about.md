I am Junteng Liu, a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am part of the HKUST NLP Group. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. I work on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Research Interests

- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability
